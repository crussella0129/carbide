Additionally: (a) `test-plan.md`'s Intent Traceability row for AC-6 lists only `verify_docs_and_metadata_accuracy`, even though the `crates/carbide-cli/src/main.rs` and About-dialog checks actually sit inside `verify_adr015_repo_urls` and AC-6's zero-regression requirement relies on `verify_workspace_fmt_clippy_test` + `verify_ui_fmt_clippy_test_wasm`; and (b) `verify_docs_and_metadata_accuracy` does not verify `T-004`'s second EARS clause (`docs/` and `docs/history/decisions-legacy.md` references) or other stale Electron-era remnants currently in `CLAUDE.md` (`Tauri`, `prettier`/`eslint`, `npm install`/`npm run dev`, and missing `native` Cargo feature from `crates/carbide-formula/Cargo.toml`).
- **C-003 — Shared-file verification ordering (`app.rs` and `CLAUDE.md`):** The dependency chain (`T-001` → `T-002` → `T-004`, with `T-003` independent) cleanly serializes overlapping edits so there are no task-boundary conflicts, and `T-001`/`T-002` touch disjoint line ranges in `apps/carbide-ui/src/app.rs`.
